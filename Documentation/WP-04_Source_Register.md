## 5. Data-Extraction Control

For every number entered into Excel, record:

- Source document
- Page number
- Financial-statement name
- Original line-item description
- Reporting unit
- Any reclassification or calculation
- Person who entered the figure
- Validation status

---

## 6. Source-Quality Assessment

The annual reports are considered primary sources because they contain the official consolidated financial statements issued by Inter IKEA Holding B.V.

The analysis will prioritize audited or formally published financial-statement information over secondary websites, articles or third-party summaries.

---

## 7. Limitations

- Inter IKEA Group is not the same entity as every IKEA retail franchise.
- Some operational information may not be disclosed by business segment.
- Certain ratios may require average balances from comparative periods.
- Financial-statement classifications may differ from those used by publicly traded Canadian companies.
- Public reports do not contain internal budgets, forecasts or customer-level transactions.
