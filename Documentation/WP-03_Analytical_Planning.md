# WP-03: Analytical Planning

## Purpose

This workpaper defines the financial analyses, calculations, dashboards and outputs that will be produced during this project.

It acts as the roadmap for the Excel financial model and Power BI dashboard.

---

# Financial Statements to Analyze

## Income Statement

Objectives

- Measure revenue growth
- Evaluate profitability
- Understand expense trends
- Identify major business changes

Key Accounts

- Operating Income
- Cost of Goods Sold
- Gross Profit
- Operating Expenses
- Operating Profit
- Net Profit

---

## Balance Sheet

- Understand capital structure

Key Accounts

- Cash
- Inventory

- Total Assets
- Total Liabilities
- Equity

## Cash Flow Statement

Objectives

# Business Questions to Validate

The financial analysis will verify whether:

- Revenue is growing sustainably.
- Profitability is improving.
- Liquidity remains healthy.
- Cash flow supports operations.
- Inventory management has improved.
- Capital structure remains stable.
- Management commentary aligns with reported financial performance.
